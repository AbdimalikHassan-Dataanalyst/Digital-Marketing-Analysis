# Digital Marketing Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes digital marketing campaign performance using interactive Power BI visualizations. It evaluates campaign spending, lead generation, customer conversations, marketing channel performance, and campaign status across different districts.

## Problem Statement
Digital marketing campaigns generate large amounts of data across multiple channels, campaign types, and geographic districts. Without proper analysis, businesses may struggle to understand how marketing budgets are being spent, which channels generate the most leads, and which campaigns produce the strongest customer engagement.

## Data
The dataset contains digital marketing campaign performance information, including:
- Campaign spending
- Lead generation
- Customer conversations
- Campaign types
- Marketing channels
- Geographic districts
- Campaign status

The dashboard summarizes total campaign spend, total leads, total conversations, and conversation rate. It also compares performance across channels, campaign types, and districts.

## Methodology
1. **Data cleaning and preparation:** Reviewed and organized campaign records, channel names, district labels, and status categories.
2. **KPI development:** Evaluated total campaign spend, total leads, total conversations, and conversation rate.
3. **Campaign performance analysis:** Compared customer conversations across campaign types and lead generation across marketing channels.
4. **Geographic analysis:** Evaluated campaign spending across districts.
5. **Dashboard development:** Built an interactive Power BI dashboard using KPI cards, bar charts, a doughnut chart, and slicers for channel and district.
6. **Insight generation:** Interpreted visual results to identify marketing performance patterns and potential opportunities for better budget allocation.

## Key Findings
- Total campaign expenditure is displayed as approximately **128K**.
- Campaigns generated approximately **40K leads**, with **Email** appearing as the leading channel in the displayed lead-generation chart.
- Approximately **14K conversations** were recorded, with a displayed conversation rate of **34%**.
- **Wadajir** shows the highest campaign spending among the districts displayed.
- The doughnut chart shows approximately **54.88%** of spending associated with pending campaigns and **45.12%** with completed campaigns.

## Business Impact
The project demonstrates how marketing data analytics can help organizations evaluate campaign effectiveness, monitor budget allocation, and understand lead generation and customer engagement across channels and districts.

The interactive dashboard provides a consolidated view of key marketing performance indicators, helping decision-makers identify high-performing channels, compare campaign spending, monitor campaign status, and identify opportunities to improve marketing efficiency and resource allocation.
